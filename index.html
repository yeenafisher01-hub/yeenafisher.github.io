Yeena Fisher

Actress • Filmmaker • Writer • Dancer

Yeena Fisher brings to life unique and compelling women—captivating, unpredictable and spontaneous, yet powerful, charismatic and unforgettable. With emotional depth and an instinct for authenticity, she creates characters that command attention and linger long after the story ends. She has worked on 66 Films and Television shows.
